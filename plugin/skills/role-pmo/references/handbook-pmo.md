Section 01

### Overview

PMO types PMI

| Type | Mandate |
|---|---|
| Supportive | Provides templates, training, lessons — low control. |
| Controlling | Requires compliance with standards & gates — medium control. |
| Directive | PMs report into the PMO; directly manages delivery — high control. |

ℹ️

Portfolio / Programme / Project = P3. The PMO governs across projects; individual project execution lives in the PM Handbook. This handbook is the governance spine's home.

PMO vs PM

|  | PMO | Project Manager |
|---|---|---|
| Scope | Whole portfolio | One project |
| Owns | Standards, governance, reporting, assurance | Delivery of scope/time/cost |
| Horizon | Continuous / strategic | Temporary / tactical |

Section 02

### Portfolio & Demand / Intake

Govern which initiatives get funded & sequenced — doing the **right** projects, not just projects right.

- **DEMAND**ideas, requests

- **INTAKE**business case

- **SCORE/RANK**value vs capacity, strategic fit

- **STAGE-GATE**Go/Kill/Hold/Recycle

- **FUND**portfolio balance

- **EXECUTE**see PM

- **CLOSE/BENEFITS**harvest value

| Intake criterion | Question |
|---|---|
| Strategic alignment | Does it advance an org objective? |
| Value / ROI | Expected benefit vs cost? |
| Risk & compliance | Regulatory / risk exposure? |
| Capacity | Do we have people to deliver it? |

🚪

**Stage-gates** (phase-gate): Go / Kill / Hold / Recycle decisions at each gate protect the portfolio from zombie projects.

Portfolio Balancing & Optimization MoP

Selection isn't just ranking — balance the portfolio across **categories** (run/grow/transform), **must-do vs discretionary** (regulatory/mandatory first), **risk profile**, and **capacity**. Seek the "efficient frontier": maximum strategic value for the available investment & delivery capacity.

Section 03

### Governance Structure

| Body | Decision rights | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| Investment / Portfolio Board | Funding, prioritization, kill decisions | Quarterly / monthly |
| Steering Committee | Phase-gate approval, major change, escalation | Monthly |
| CAB | Change/release risk (see Deployment) | Weekly |
| PMO Operations | Standards, reporting, assurance | Continuous |

📏

Clear **decision rights** & escalation thresholds prevent bottlenecks. Tailor governance weight to project size & risk.

Section 04

### RAID Management

The consolidated **RAID log** is the PMO's central control instrument across the portfolio.

| Letter | Item | Tracks |
|---|---|---|
| R | Risks | Potential future problems — probability × impact, owner, response. |
| A | Assumptions | Things taken as true — validate; an invalid assumption becomes a risk/issue. |
| I | Issues | Problems happening now — owner, action, due date. |
| D | Dependencies | Cross-project / external reliances — see Section 05. |

🔗

Project-level risk practice lives in the PM Handbook; the PMO **aggregates** RAID to a portfolio view & manages cross-cutting items.

🔗

Section 05

### Dependency & Cross-Project Management

- Maintain a **dependency map** across projects (who needs what, by when).

- Classify: internal vs external, hard vs soft, inbound vs outbound.

- Sequence & align milestones; flag critical-path dependencies to the boards early.

- Resolve conflicts on shared resources/systems at the portfolio level, not project vs project.

💰

Section 06

### Financial Governance

Govern the money: business case → cost baseline → tracking → forecast. **Earned Value Management (EVM)** for objective progress:

| Metric | Formula | Reads as |
|---|---|---|
| CPI | EV ÷ AC | Cost efficiency (>1 = under budget) |
| SPI | EV ÷ PV | Schedule efficiency (>1 = ahead) |
| CV | EV − AC | Cost variance |
| EAC | BAC ÷ CPI | Forecast total cost at completion |

EV earned value · AC actual cost · PV planned value · BAC budget at completion

🎁

Section 07

### Benefits Realization

Projects deliver outputs; the organization needs **outcomes & benefits**. The PMO ensures value is actually harvested — not assumed at go-live.

- **IDENTIFY**benefits in the business case

- **PLAN**owner, baseline, KPI

- **(deliver)**

- **REALIZE**measure actual vs target

- **REVIEW**benefits review (3–6 mo post go-live)

🔗

Each benefit has an **owner, baseline, target KPI, and review date**. Ties to the PO outcome metrics and the Implementation Handbook closure (PIR).

📚

Section 08

### Delivery Standards & Methodology

- Own the delivery framework (this handbook set), templates, and quality gates (DoR/DoD, phase gates).

- **Tailoring guidance:** right-size Waterfall/Agile/Hybrid to project size, risk, and regulatory scope.

- Curate the **template library** (charter, business case, status, RAID, closure) and lessons-learned repository.

- Champion consistency across the PM, PO, BA, SA, QC handbooks.

- Curate the **lessons-learned repository** — captured at each closure (PIR) and fed back into standards & intake criteria.

🔄

**Organizational Change Management (OCM):** delivery outputs only create value if people adopt them. Plan the people side with **ADKAR** (Awareness · Desire · Knowledge · Ability · Reinforcement) Prosci — stakeholder comms, training, and adoption tracking — alongside technical delivery. Section 09

### Resource & Capacity Management

- Portfolio-level view of demand vs capacity; prevent over-allocation across projects.

- Skills matrix & capacity heatmap; forecast hiring/vendor needs.

- Resolve competing resource claims via portfolio priority, not first-come-first-served.

🔍

Section 10

### Assurance & Audit

Independent checks that delivery is on track & controls are working P3O:

- **Health checks / gate reviews** at phase boundaries; RAG-rate delivery confidence.

- Compliance with governance & regulatory controls (with Security & Compliance).

- Track audit findings & actions to closure; feed lessons back into standards.

📊

Section 11

### Reporting & Portfolio Dashboard

A single, consistent view of portfolio health for executives — **RAG** status with drill-down.

| Layer | Report |
|---|---|
| Executive | Portfolio scorecard: RAG, spend vs budget, benefits, top risks. |
| Programme | Milestones, cross-project dependencies, RAID summary. |
| Project | Status report (from PM): scope/schedule/cost, EVM, issues. |

🎨

RAG: 🟢 on track · 🟡 at risk (mitigation in progress) · 🔴 off track (escalation). Consistent definitions across all projects — no "watermelon" reporting (green outside, red inside).

📈

Section 12

### Portfolio KPIs

On-time delivery

projects on schedule ÷ total × 100%

On-budget delivery

avg CPI ≥ 0.95 across portfolio

Benefits realized

actual ÷ forecast benefit × 100%

Portfolio ROI

net benefit ÷ investment × 100%

Resource utilization

billable/allocated ÷ capacity × 100%

Gate throughput

initiatives passing each gate
