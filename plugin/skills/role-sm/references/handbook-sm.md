Section 01

### Overview

Accountability Scrum 2020

The Scrum Master is **accountable for establishing Scrum** as defined in the Scrum Guide, and for the **Scrum Team's effectiveness**. The SM is a **true leader who serves** the Scrum Team and the wider organization — one of the three accountabilities (SM, PO, Developers).

The SM serves three ways: the **Scrum Team** (coaching, facilitating, removing impediments), the **Product Owner** (backlog & goal techniques), and the **Organization** (adoption, removing barriers between stakeholders & teams).

SM vs PM vs Agile Coach vs Team Lead

| Aspect | Scrum Master | Project Manager | Agile Coach | Team Lead |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Focus | Team effectiveness & Scrum | Scope/time/cost of a project | Org-wide agility | Technical delivery of a team |
| Authority | No command authority — influence | Formal project authority | Advisory across teams | Line/technical authority |
| Horizon | The team, continuous | The project, temporary | Multiple teams | The team's codebase |

ℹ️

SM is **not** a mini-PM. See the PM Handbook for project governance and the PO Handbook for product decisions.

Section 02

### Scrum Framework Scrum 2020

| Element | Items |
|---|---|
| Accountabilities | Product Owner · Scrum Master · Developers not "roles" |
| Events | The Sprint (container) · Sprint Planning · Daily Scrum · Sprint Review · Sprint Retrospective |
| Artifacts + commitment | Product Backlog → Product Goal · Sprint Backlog → Sprint Goal · Increment → Definition of Done |
| Pillars | Transparency · Inspection · Adaptation (empiricism) |
| Values | Commitment · Focus · Openness · Respect · Courage |

⚠️

Use current terms: **accountabilities** (not "3 roles"), **Developers** (not "Development Team"). Refinement is an **ongoing activity**, not an event.

Section 03

### Servant Leadership

The SM leads by **serving** — creating the conditions for the team to succeed, not commanding it. Robert Greenleaf

- Grow people & the team's self-management; make yourself progressively less needed.

- Protect focus (shield from interruptions), foster **psychological safety**.

- Lead change by influence & example, not authority.

- Hold the team to Scrum values & agreements without dictating how they work.

🔁

Section 04

### Facilitating the Events Scrum 2020

| Event | SM ensures / does | SM does NOT |
|---|---|---|
| Sprint Planning | Event happens & is productive; time-box respected; Sprint Goal is clear. | Decide scope or assign tasks. |
| Daily Scrum | Coach Developers to run it themselves in 15 min. | Run it or take status. |
| Sprint Review | Facilitate collaboration with stakeholders; keep it a working session. | Present the product (that's the team). |
| Retrospective | Facilitate honest inspection & concrete improvements. | Impose actions or skip it. |
| Refinement ongoing | Help the team make it effective & right-sized. | Own the backlog (PO does). |

ℹ️

SM facilitation is a **service**, not a requirement — a mature team may need less. The goal is a self-managing team.

🚧

Section 05

### Impediment Management

An **impediment** is anything slowing the team that they can't remove themselves. The SM causes their removal.

- **SURFACE**daily, retro

- **LOG**visible board

- **CLASSIFY**team-solvable? org-level?

- **ACT/ESCALATE**remove or escalate to mgmt/PM

- **VERIFY REMOVED**with the team

Team-level → coach the team to solve. Organizational → escalate (to PM/management) with impact framed clearly. Track impediments visibly to closure.

🎓

Section 06

### Coaching Stances

Choose the right stance for the situation Agile coaching:

| Stance | When |
|---|---|
| Teaching | Team lacks knowledge of Scrum/practice. |
| Mentoring | Sharing experience on a specific challenge. |
| Facilitating | Team has the answers — guide the process, stay content-neutral. |
| Coaching | Unlock the team's own thinking via powerful questions. |

💬

**GROW model** John Whitmore: Goal → Reality → Options → Will. Ask, don't tell — build the team's capability.

🗣️

Section 07

### Facilitation Toolkit

- **Working agreements** & a clear purpose for every session.

- Divergent → convergent flow; timeboxing; parking lot for off-topic items.

- Techniques: dot voting, fist-of-five, silent brainstorming, round-robin, Liberating Structures.

- Draw out quiet voices; manage dominators; stay content-neutral as facilitator.

- Decision methods: consent, consensus, or advice — chosen explicitly.

👥

Section 08

### Team Formation & Health

**Tuckman stages** Bruce Tuckman: Forming → Storming → Norming → Performing → (Adjourning). The SM adapts support to the stage — more structure early, more autonomy later.

- **Psychological safety** Amy Edmondson is the #1 predictor of team performance (Google Project Aristotle).

- Foster stable, cross-functional, right-sized teams (Scrum: typically ≤10 people).

- Watch team-health signals (mood, conflict, dependency, churn); act early.

📊

Section 09

### Agile Metrics — Use & Abuse

| Metric | Good use | Abuse |
|---|---|---|
| Velocity | Team's own capacity forecast | Comparing teams / as a target |
| Burndown / Burnup | In-sprint transparency | Micromanagement stick |
| Cumulative Flow (CFD) | Spot bottlenecks & WIP | — |
| Cycle / Lead time | Improve flow (see PO) | — |
| Team happiness / safety | Health trend over time | Individual scoring |

⚠️

Metrics are for the **team to inspect & adapt**, never a management weapon (Goodhart's law). Outcomes > output.

| EBM Key Value Area Scrum.org | Asks |
|---|---|
| Current Value (CV) | Value delivered to customers/users today. |
| Unrealized Value (UV) | Potential future value if needs are met. |
| Time-to-Market (T2M) | How fast the team learns & delivers. |
| Ability-to-Innovate (A2I) | Capacity to deliver new value (vs toil/tech-debt drag). |

🏢

**Serving the organization:** maintain an **org-level impediment backlog** and foster a **Community of Practice** — the SM removes systemic blockers beyond the single team.

Section 10

### Scaling Extended

| Framework | Idea |
|---|---|
| Nexus Scrum.org | 3–9 Scrum Teams on one product; Nexus Integration Team manages cross-team dependencies. |
| LeSS | Scale Scrum with minimal added process; one PO, one backlog. |
| SAFe | Enterprise ART/PI planning; SM often "Scrum Master / Team Coach". |

At scale, SMs also facilitate **Scrum of Scrums** / cross-team sync — coordinate with the PM on dependencies.

🔄

Section 11

### Retrospective Techniques

Vary the format to keep retros fresh & honest Derby & Larsen:

- **Start / Stop / Continue** — simple, action-focused.

- **4 Ls** — Liked, Learned, Lacked, Longed for.

- **Mad / Sad / Glad** — surface emotion.

- **Sailboat** — wind (helps) vs anchors (blockers).

- **5-stage**: Set the stage → Gather data → Generate insights → Decide actions → Close.

✅

Every retro ends with **1–3 concrete, owned action items** — and the next retro reviews them. No actions = theatre.

🚫

Section 12

### Anti-Patterns

| Anti-pattern | Do instead |
|---|---|
| SM as secretary (just books rooms, takes notes) | Coach, facilitate, remove impediments. |
| SM as PM (assigns tasks, tracks status) | Enable self-management; PM owns project governance. |
| "Scrum police" (dogmatic rule enforcer) | Teach the why; adapt with the team. |
| Absent / part-time token SM | Actively serve the team & org. |
| Velocity as a KPI | Focus on outcomes & team health. |
